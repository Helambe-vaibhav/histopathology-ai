🧪 Pathologist & Computer Vision Synthesis: Which One is Better?

Histopathology images represent multi-component biological tissue stained by chemical dyes. Determining "which one is better" depends on the analytical objective:

1. For Isolating Nuclear Chromatin: Hematoxylin Deconvolution is scientifically superior. Standard grayscale merges pink eosin with purple nuclei; optical density unmixing completely eliminates the pink eosin channel, boosting nuclear CNR to 1.38.
2. For Zero-Cost Single Channel Input: Green Channel Extraction is the best raw camera channel. Hematoxylin has peak optical extinction in the green band (~560nm), delivering 20% higher nuclear contrast than the red channel.
3. For Membrane Edge Preservation: Bilateral Filter outperforms Gaussian blur. Gaussian blur softens cell borders and merges touching nuclei. Bilateral filtering smooths intracellular chromatin noise while keeping membrane transitions sharp.
4. The Definitive Winner: Golden Composite Pipeline (H-Deconv + Bilateral + CLAHE). Achieves the highest Otsu Dice (0.841), IoU (0.725), CNR (1.64), and Fisher separation (2.69). It outperforms all single filters and raw channels across every metric.
